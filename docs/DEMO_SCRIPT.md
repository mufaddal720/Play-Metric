# Demonstration script (about 6 minutes)

**Setup before the judges arrive:** a laptop or tablet on a stand at about hip height, 3 m from a clear space; a second phone or tripod camera high to the side of a small pitch (two mini goals, a bright orange ball); good light; the app open at the public URL; history cleared (Settings).

### 0. Introduction (30 s)
"This is UAE Family AI Game Day: three family games in one app. The AI watches real gameplay and measures what happened. A rules engine then turns those measurements into points, and every point comes with evidence and an explanation. Everything runs on this device; no video is uploaded." Point out the UAE time clock and the privacy badge.

### 1. Setup (30 s)
Home → **Play** → add two players (e.g. *Mariam*, *Hamdan*). Show individual/team mode, team renaming, difficulty and timings.

### 2. Freeze & Fool (90 s)
1. Classic, 2 rounds, medium. Start. Point out the skeletons and name labels over each player ("this is pose tracking, not face recognition").
2. Round 1: both copy the pose properly → verdict: show the 60/25/15 bars, open **Evidence & explanations**, and read one explanation aloud ("the left forearm was 12° from the target").
3. Round 2: one player copies, the other deliberately keeps wiggling → stability drops, and the explanation says so.
4. *(Optional honesty demo)* One player steps out of frame during FREEZE → **Needs review, no score** → press **Retake round**.

### 3. AI Football (2 min)
1. New setup → AI Football, 2 minutes, restart "Automatic".
2. Calibration: tap the pitch corners, then each goal's posts and inside point, then tap the ball. Show the **ball tracking test** (detection rate, size, frames/s).
3. Kick-off. Score a goal → the scoreboard changes only after **"GOAL! verified by the AI"**. Open the event: tracked approach, crossing point on the goal mouth, ball inside.
4. Shoot wide of the post → nothing is counted. Let the ball rest near the line → nothing is counted.
5. Block the camera with a hand while shooting → **"Possible goal needs host review"**. Use **Host review → Count this goal**, type a reason, and show it labelled *Host reviewed* with the original AI result kept.

### 4. Action Detective (90 s)
1. Setup → Action Detective, easy, 1 turn each.
2. Hand the device to the performer; everyone looks away; **hold to reveal**.
3. The performer acts. A guesser types a wrong guess, then the right one → +15.
4. Verdict: the answer is revealed, along with the AI-observed action and the cue checklist with percentages. The performer's points (AI match + cues) are kept separate from the guesser's 15.

### 5. Results & close (30 s)
Results page: winner or draw, round-by-round breakdown, achievements (each with the recorded reason), host-reviewed items labelled, times in GST. "The AI never invents a score: when it is not sure, it says so and asks a human."
